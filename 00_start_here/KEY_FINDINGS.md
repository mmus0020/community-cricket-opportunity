# Key Findings

- **365** sufficiently sized LGAs were included in Model V1.
- **17** were classified as Very High Opportunity.
- **18** LGAs remained in the Top 20 under every tested weighting scenario.
- Wyndham ranked #1 under the Balanced Model, followed by Melton, Blacktown, Ipswich and Camden.
- Total population and absolute junior-market size are almost redundant ranking signals (Spearman ≈ **0.99**).
- Overall population growth and junior-population growth are related but distinct (Spearman ≈ **0.57**).
- **94.6%** of the Top-20 absolute increase in 5–14-year-olds was concentrated across four broad growth systems:
  - outer Melbourne;
  - western/south-west Sydney;
  - South East Queensland;
  - Perth growth belt.
- Phase 14 local evidence suggests different management responses:
  - **Wyndham:** capacity audit + targeted acquisition;
  - **Blacktown:** potential capacity-constrained priority;
  - **Ipswich:** activate around new capacity;
  - **Armadale:** validate demand before scaling;
  - **Maitland:** regional growth activation.
- The main strategic recommendation is to build an **Opportunity × Readiness** prioritisation system using internal registration, club/program and facility-capacity data.
