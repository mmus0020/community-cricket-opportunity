# Phase 3 — Cricket Australia Strategy & Targets

The study was aligned to Australian Cricket's participation priorities, particularly:
- junior participation growth;
- women and girls;
- multicultural inclusion;
- community facilities and delivery capacity.

Published strategic context reviewed during the project included Australian Cricket Strategy 2022–2027, the Women & Girls Action Plan, Multicultural Action Plan, Cricket Census reporting and state-association participation updates.

These targets inform strategic interpretation, but they are not treated as model outcomes.
