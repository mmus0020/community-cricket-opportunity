# Phase 7 — Hypotheses

The project established 22 hypotheses before model construction to reduce post-hoc storytelling.

Primary hypotheses included:
- opportunity-related demographic conditions vary materially across LGAs;
- junior-market growth contains information beyond total-population growth;
- absolute junior size and junior share identify different places;
- no single demographic indicator captures opportunity;
- raw population measures are correlated and can double-count scale;
- percentage growth is volatile for small markets;
- high-opportunity LGAs may have low System Readiness;
- Opportunity × Readiness segmentation should be more actionable than ranking alone.

Hypothesis outcomes are tracked in `04_data/model_outputs/phase10_eda__hypothesis_status_phase10.csv`.
