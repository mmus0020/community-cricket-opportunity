# Phase 6 — Analytical Framework

## Core components
1. Addressable Junior Market
2. Growth Momentum
3. Strategic Participation Alignment
4. Cricket System Readiness
5. Context & Constraints

## Locked rule
**Cricket supply is not part of Opportunity Potential.** Opportunity and Readiness are separate axes.

## Intended final strategic matrix
- High opportunity / high readiness → Activate / Accelerate
- High opportunity / low readiness → Build Capacity
- Lower opportunity / high readiness → Optimise / Retain
- Lower opportunity / low readiness → Monitor / Selective Investment

Model V1 is intentionally an opportunity-side prototype.
