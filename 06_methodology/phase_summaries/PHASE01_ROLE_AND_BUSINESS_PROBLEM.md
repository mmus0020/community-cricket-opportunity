# Phase 1 — Role & Business Problem

## Core business problem
How can Australian Cricket use fragmented participation, customer, demographic and research data to decide **where to invest, whom to target, how to retain participants, and whether participation strategies are working?**

## Role-relevant analytical problems
1. Retention and churn
2. Acquisition
3. Consistent national reporting and KPIs
4. Resource allocation
5. Strategy effectiveness

## Project decision
Build a public-data prototype that identifies where community-cricket acquisition investigation should be prioritised, while explicitly avoiding claims that public data can measure individual churn or true local participation penetration.
