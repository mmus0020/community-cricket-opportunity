# Phase 2 — Research Question & Scope

## Primary research question
**Which Australian communities show the strongest potential for community cricket participation growth, and what factors should Cricket Australia and State/Territory Cricket Associations consider when prioritising participation-acquisition initiatives?**

## Unit of analysis
Local Government Area (LGA), Australia-wide.

## Target groups
General participation, children/young people, women/girls and culturally diverse communities.

## Out of scope
Individual churn prediction, professional-cricket performance, exact causal claims, ROI forecasts and exact participant forecasts.

## Decision use
If acquisition resources are limited, identify which communities deserve deeper investigation first and why.
