# Data Attribution

Core demographic data used in this project was sourced from the **Australian Bureau of Statistics (ABS)**, including:
- Regional Population;
- Regional Population by Age and Sex;
- Socio-Economic Indexes for Areas (SEIFA);
- Local Government Area geography standards.

Official source workbooks are retained in `raw_abs/` for transparency and reproducibility.

Additional contextual evidence was drawn from:
- Cricket Australia / Australian Cricket strategy and participation reporting;
- Australian Sports Commission / AusPlay;
- Cricket Victoria;
- Cricket NSW;
- Queensland Cricket;
- WA Cricket;
- local governments for case-study infrastructure evidence.

See `source_registers/` for the collected source registers.

ABS content is generally published under Australian Government open-data licensing/Creative Commons terms, but users should verify the current licence on the source page before redistribution or reuse. This repository makes no claim of ownership over third-party data.
